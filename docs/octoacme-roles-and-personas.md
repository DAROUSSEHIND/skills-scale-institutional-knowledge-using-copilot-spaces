# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA / Test Engineers validate whether the delivered solution meets acceptance criteria, quality expectations, and release readiness requirements. They help the team catch defects early and reduce delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage, and test plans for features and releases
- Validate acceptance criteria through functional, regression, integration, and end-to-end testing
- Partner with Developers on testability, defect triage, and issue prioritization
- Report quality risks, release blockers, and confidence levels to the PM and Product Manager
- Support readiness reviews for major milestones and production release gates

### Goals
- Improve confidence in product quality and release readiness
- Reduce defects escaping into production
- Align validation work with business and user expectations

### Typical Collaboration
- Works closely with Developers on bug reproduction, test coverage, and quality fixes
- Confirms expected behavior with Product Managers during backlog definition and acceptance reviews
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, QA windows, and risk communication

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, defect reports, and risk summaries
- QA readiness checklists and release validation updates
- Cross-functional review meetings for release and milestone signoff

---

## Designer / UX Researcher

### Role Summary
Designers and UX Researchers bring user needs, interaction design, and accessibility considerations into discovery and delivery. They help translate user problems into feasible, testable solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize insights for product decisions
- Create user flows, wireframes, and interaction patterns that support usability
- Validate accessibility, clarity, and consistency of the user experience
- Collaborate with Product Managers to refine problem statements and success measures
- Partner with Developers and QA to ensure implementation matches intended behavior

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction
- Ensure the product solves the right problem in a way users can understand and trust
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements or design assumptions

### Typical Collaboration
- Works with Product Managers to shape problems, opportunity areas, and success measures
- Works with Developers to ensure feasible and accessible implementation
- Works with QA to validate user-facing behavior and edge cases

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- User journey documentation and UX decisions
- Feedback loops tied to acceptance criteria and validation

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Engineering Leads guide technical direction, delivery quality, and cross-team engineering coordination. They help balance technical trade-offs with product needs and execution realities.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture, implementation approach, and engineering standards
- Help estimate effort, identify dependencies, and manage technical risk
- Support design and code reviews to maintain quality and consistency
- Coordinate engineering execution across teams or workstreams
- Escalate major technical risks, resource constraints, or delivery concerns to PMs and Product Leads

### Goals
- Deliver robust, maintainable systems that meet product needs
- Improve team execution quality and technical consistency
- Reduce architecture drift and avoid avoidable delivery risk

### Typical Collaboration
- Partners with Developers on implementation quality and review standards
- Coordinates with Project Managers and Product Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and trade-offs
- Provides technical risk input during planning, release, and escalation discussions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture decisions and technical trade-off reviews
- Dependency tracking and planning updates
- Risk communication tied to milestones, delivery health, and technical debt

---

## Release / Operations (SRE or DevOps)

### Role Summary
Release and Operations roles ensure deployments are safe, reliable, and observable. They support production readiness, monitoring, rollback planning, and operational handoff.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare deployment pipelines, environment readiness, and release checklists
- Monitor service health, reliability, and production alerts
- Support smoke tests, deployment verification, and rollback readiness
- Coordinate release windows and operational handoff with engineering and PM teams
- Identify infrastructure or operational risks that affect delivery and customer impact

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and production instability
- Improve service reliability and operational visibility
- Support smooth, predictable deployments and incident response

### Typical Collaboration
- Coordinates with Developers and QA on release validation and deployment readiness
- Works with Project Managers on release timing, rollback plans, and stakeholder communication
- Helps Product Managers assess customer impact and communicate operational considerations

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness checks and deployment status
- Monitoring and post-deploy validation summaries
- Incident and rollback communications when needed

---

## Security / Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy partners help identify and mitigate risks related to data protection, compliance, and secure system design. They support teams in building safe and trusted products.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements, designs, and implementation choices for security and privacy concerns
- Identify vulnerabilities, data handling risks, and compliance obligations
- Partner with Developers and Release/Operations on mitigation and validation
- Escalate material concerns through project risk and incident pathways
- Support secure release decisions and post-incident review when relevant

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk throughout the lifecycle
- Improve trust and compliance without slowing delivery unnecessarily
- Ensure critical concerns are addressed early and transparently

### Typical Collaboration
- Works with Developers on secure design, remediation, and testing
- Collaborates with Release/Operations on deployment safeguards and security signoff
- Escalates material risks through the PM and sponsor pathways when needed

### Typical Communication
- Security review summaries, risk assessments, and remediation plans
- Privacy and compliance considerations during planning and release
- Incident-related escalation updates and follow-up actions

---

## Business Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
The Business Sponsor provides strategic alignment, executive support, and decisions on escalated trade-offs. They help ensure the project remains connected to business priorities and organizational goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic fit and organizational alignment
- Support prioritization decisions involving scope, resources, or timing trade-offs
- Help resolve escalated blockers that require executive sponsorship
- Provide stakeholder alignment and sponsorship for major milestones or changes
- Ensure the project remains aligned with broader business outcomes

### Goals
- Protect strategic value and business momentum
- Enable timely decision-making when trade-offs are required
- Support delivery of measurable outcomes that matter to the organization

### Typical Collaboration
- Works with Project Managers and Product Managers on key decisions and escalations
- Supports stakeholder alignment without replacing day-to-day project ownership
- Helps address cross-functional dependencies or conflicts across business units

### Typical Communication
- Steering updates, milestone decisions, and risk escalations
- Executive briefings and strategic checkpoints
- Decision support for changes in scope, timing, or resourcing

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Not every project needs every persona; use the roles that align to the scope, delivery model, risk profile, and stakeholder structure of the initiative.
